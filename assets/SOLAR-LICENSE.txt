Maia's interface icons are the Solar icon set by 480 Design
(https://www.figma.com/community/file/1166831539721848736), licensed under the Creative
Commons Attribution 4.0 International License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

They were taken from https://github.com/saoudi-h/solar-icons (commit 24f6ff1, October 2026),
which publishes the collection under the MIT licence below. Changes made for Maia: the icons'
colour is replaced with white so that Maia can tint them, their coordinates are rounded to
two decimal places, and they are drawn at the sizes Maia shows them. The eject and wrap icons
are Maia's own, drawn in the same style.
